About me

who I am and what I work on

I'm a Mechanical Engineering graduate (B.Tech with Honours, Minor in Electronics and Communication) from IIT Ropar.

My research interests include robotics, mechanical simulations, evolutionary game theory, and artificial intelligence. Much of my work lies at the intersection of these fields, and I am always eager to learn new things, explore new ideas, and take on challenging problems. If you are interested in collaborating on any of these topics, feel free to reach out!

Beyond academics and research, I enjoy exploring emerging technologies and contributing to open-source projects. I love playing basketball, football, and chess. I am fluent in English, Hindi, and Marathi, have a basic understanding of Spanish and German, and am working towards learning Dutch and perhaps a few more languages along the way. I also play the piano and tabla, and enjoy hiking, discovering new places, and spending time with friends.
